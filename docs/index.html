Privacy Policy

Croqui is an offline app. It does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in. There is no login.
  • No network. Croqui works fully offline. It does not send any data to us or to any third party, and it contains no analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  • Your data stays on your device. Your learning progress, streak, and settings are saved only in Croqui's local storage on your device. We never see them.
  • You are in control. You can erase your progress at any time from Settings → Data → Reset progress. Uninstalling the app removes all of its data from your device.
  • Children's privacy. Because Croqui collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. It is safe for learners of any age.

If a future version adds any feature that uses the internet, this policy will be updated and that feature will be clearly optional.
